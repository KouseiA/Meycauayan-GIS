User Journey

How MEYCMAP Works

Simple 3-step workflow designed to save time during critical situations.

1

Detect Location

Tap the GPS button to detect your current position, or pick your barangay from the 26-barangay directory.

2

Filter Service

Select Medical, Fire, Police, or Health Center to filter the map and highlight the nearest facility instantly.

3

Route & Call

View shortest route in meters, check travel time estimates, and tap the 1-touch Speed-Dial to contact responders.
