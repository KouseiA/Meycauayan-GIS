Civilian Emergency Flow

How It Works in 3 Simple Steps

Designed for intuitive clarity when panic and crisis strike.

01

Pin or Detect Location

Tap the GPS locator button to detect your current position, or select your barangay from the comprehensive 26-barangay directory to focus the map.

02

Filter Emergency Type

Choose your emergency category: Medical Emergency, Fire Hazard, Police Assistance, or Disaster Evacuation Shelter. Only relevant facilities are highlighted.

03

Dispatch & Speed-Dial

Instantly review the distance in meters, turn-by-turn navigation directions, and press the direct Speed-Dial button to connect with emergency responders.
